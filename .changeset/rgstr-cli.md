The `rgstr` CLI: `login` / `logout` / `whoami` (device-code sign-in, `RGSTR_TOKEN` for CI), `use [--off]` (project `.npmrc` / pip config, byte-for-byte undo), `claim [--check] [--badge]` (file the claim and write the publish-proof token, then verify it from the published package), `whois`, `route`, and `search`.
